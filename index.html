What we do

Two practices. Equal weight. One focus.

Research tells a zone what to change. Technology advisory helps it make the change.

Practice 1

Research

Independent evidence on how zones perform, compete and grow.

  • Flagship studiesThe yearly Zone Outlook, plus studies on law, tax, trade and talent. Free to read.
  • Zone database and benchmarksEvery zone in one common format, compared with its true peers.
  • Policy and legal frameworksZone laws, incentives and governance that fit international rules.
  • Custom researchFeasibility, market and impact studies for a zone or a government.
Practice 2

Technology advisory

Practical advice on the systems that make a zone fast, digital and easy to do business in.

  • Digital zone strategyA technology roadmap tied to the zone’s growth plan and budget.
  • Investor platformsOne-stop online services for licences, visas, customs and client care.
  • AI and dataUse cases, data foundations and quick pilots for zone teams and tenants.
  • Vendor selection and deliveryClear requirements, fair vendor choice and oversight until go-live.
